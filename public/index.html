The idea

Fast enough to stay
out of your way.

Calling a language model after every letter adds a round trip to every thought. Typer takes a different approach: AI ranks your document’s vocabulary in the background, then a local engine handles the next keystroke.

The suggestion appears as gray text. Keep typing, dismiss it, or press Tab. Familiar phrases can continue when the evidence is strong. Typer never inserts anything on its own.

Install in Chrome or Edge

Download and unzip the extension. Open your browser’s Extensions page, enable Developer mode, and choose “Load unpacked”. Select the unzipped folder containing manifest.json, then refresh your writing page.

Supports ordinary text inputs, textareas, and contenteditable editors. Google Docs, canvas editors, closed shadow roots, and browser-protected pages aren’t supported in this release.
