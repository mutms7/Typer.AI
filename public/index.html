Just a little faster.

When you write an email, take notes, or work through an idea, the thought should come from you. But typing the same words again and again can slow you down.

Typer learns the words in your document and offers the rest in gray. Press Tab when it’s what you meant. Keep typing when it isn’t. You choose the words, the direction, and the voice.

Cloud-connected versions periodically rank your document’s vocabulary with AI. The next keystroke is handled locally, so your writing never has to wait for a model to answer.

Install in Chrome or Edge +

Download and unzip the extension. Open your browser’s Extensions page, enable Developer mode, and choose “Load unpacked”. Select the folder containing manifest.json, then refresh your writing page.

Works with ordinary text inputs, textareas, and editable web documents. Google Docs and canvas editors aren’t supported in this release. The current download runs locally without an account or API key.
